Quolin.
Crypto, the careful way · Two-page summary · October 2026

Quolin is a crypto investing app built for people who don't trust crypto, and sold through the banks they do trust. It gets to know each client the way an adviser would, shows them only what is right for them, sets a personal limit, and keeps a record a regulator can check years later.

Half the problem is who stands between you and the coins. Scams, no recovery and no protection are what a bank already fixes for savings: a name, a helpline, a password reset, a regulator.
The other half is how crypto is presented. It is sold like a casino. Quolin presents it the way Vanguard presents index funds: a few assets, explained plainly, sized to what you can afford to lose.

The rules behind every outcome come from the Quolin engine already built: jurisdiction rule packs, allow/block/refer with reasons, and a tamper-evident record that can be replayed. The bank signs off every rule and owns every client decision.

06Cross-border: where we start

1 · Lead
Singapore
First bank pilot. The Risk Awareness Assessment has been required since June 2025, and our SG rule pack already exists. Tokenised T-bills are allowed for retail. Su-Lee's network is here.
2 · Scale
EU
Fastest way in for banks. Under MiCA Art. 60 a bank can offer crypto by notifying its regulator. No new licence, one rulebook for 27 countries, and banks are launching now (BBVA, Raiffeisen, Sparkassen).
3 · Ready
UK · HK
UK regime goes live 25 Oct 2027, with applications open now: pitch "ready on day one". HK requires a personal limit per client, which is our core mechanic. Then AU, MY and US through existing contacts.
How one app goes cross-border: each country is a rule pack (who may buy, which assets, test, limits, cooling-off) plus a currency layer. The client sees their home currency; prices come from live FX. When two countries' rules apply, the engine says which governs, or refers the case.
